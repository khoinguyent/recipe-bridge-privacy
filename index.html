Privacy Policy

Updated October 14, 2026

Overview

Recipe Bridge develops Fujifilm RAW photos and film recipes on this device. Photos, recipes, and edits stay on the device. The app has no account and does not upload your photos to a server.

Photos

The app reads RAW files you choose from the Photos library or the Files app so you can apply a recipe. When you save or import a picture, it is written to your Photos library, including an album named Recipe Bridge. Those files are not sent off the device by this app.

Camera

The camera is used only when you scan a printed or on-screen recipe. The scan is read on this device to fill in recipe settings.

Local network

If you connect to a Fujifilm camera over its Wi-Fi network, the app talks to the camera on that local network to import photos or send a recipe. That traffic stays between this device and the camera.

Purchases

Pro is billed by Apple as a yearly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment. The app stores on this device whether Pro is active, and how many free recipe imports you have used. It does not receive your payment card details.

What is not collected

The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not include advertising or analytics tracking.
